## General Tips for Tech Internship Applications
1. Apply 3-6 months before your desired start date
2. Customize your CV for each application
3. Prepare a GitHub portfolio with clean, documented projects
4. Practice coding challenges on LeetCode or HackerRank
5. Research the company before interviews
6. Network on LinkedIn with company employees
7. Follow up after submitting applications
8. Prepare questions to ask the interviewer
